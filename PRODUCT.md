# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Staff at small nonprofits and ministries (1–5 people, typically under 5,000 donors) who manage donors, receipts, and bookkeeping alongside everything else. Non-technical; often working out of QuickBooks daily. Their context is task-driven: log a gift, thank a donor, plan a visit, close the books.

## Product Purpose

Vantage is an AI-powered donor CRM that replaces manual data entry with intelligent automation. It surfaces who to thank, who to visit, and who's slipping away — and keeps QuickBooks in sync automatically. Success looks like a one-person development office that runs like a five-person one.

## Brand Personality

Confident, clear, steadfast. Speaks in outcomes, not data. Calm institutional-modern — a well-kept ledger with a sharp eye — never SaaS-flashy.

## Anti-references

- Enterprise-CRM bloat: Salesforce-style density, configuration sprawl, features that need an administrator.
- The generic SaaS gradient-accent look (Vantage's retired green→cyan gradient included).
- Dashboards that celebrate data instead of prompting action.

## Design Principles

1. **Outcomes over data** — every surface should answer "what should I do next," not just report numbers.
2. **Earned familiarity** — shadcn vocabulary, standard affordances; the tool disappears into the task.
3. **The AI is an accent, not a costume** — the green→cyan logo gradient is reserved for AI moments; everything else stays quiet.
4. **Both themes are first-class** — light and dark are designed, not derived by accident.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Text contrast ≥4.5:1 (≥3:1 for large text); chart palettes machine-validated for CVD separation and 3:1 contrast in both themes; multi-series charts always keep legends/tooltips as secondary encoding. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored for all animation.
